Simple, transparent admission process

Begin your child’s learning journey at ST XAVIERS INTERNATIONAL SCHOOL with a guided step-by-step process.

Admission Steps

  1. Enquiry and school visit with counselling support
  2. Application form submission with student details
  3. Document verification at admission desk
  4. Interaction/assessment as per class level
  5. Fee payment and seat confirmation

Essential Documents

  • Birth certificate copy
  • Previous report card
  • Transfer certificate (if applicable)
  • Parent ID/address proof
  • Passport-size photographs
Admission Window

Admissions are processed as per seat availability and school schedule.

Parent Guidance

Our team supports parents through form filling and policy clarifications.

Admission Helpdesk

For deadlines and fee details, contact the school office during working hours.
